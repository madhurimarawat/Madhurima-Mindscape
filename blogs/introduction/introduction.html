Welcome to Madhurima Mindscape, a personal space for my thoughts, experiences, and interests. I am Madhurima Rawat, a B.Tech (Hons.) graduate in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science), exploring the things I learn, create, and enjoy along the way.

Welcome to My Personal Space!

This is my personal blog, where I share reflections, life experiences, things I am learning, and occasional tech-related explorations. It is a little corner of the internet where I can express myself beyond a résumé or portfolio.

How It All Started

I wanted a space to share my thoughts and experiences in a way that felt more personal than GitHub and less formal than LinkedIn. That is how this blog came to be: a place for the things I think about, the things I discover, and the experiences I want to write about.

I also wanted the website itself to feel different from the polished, minimal, almost perfect-looking websites we see everywhere online. There is nothing wrong with that kind of design, but I feel like perfection can get a little boring when everything starts looking the same. I wanted this space to have a personality of its own, even if that meant being a little tacky or cliché at times.

The gradients and bright colors came from that idea, especially the yellow-and-pink combination. Honestly, that gradient was one of the first things I had in mind when I started designing the site. I also drew inspiration from Ocean Blue and a bunch of other colors, ideas, and little things I came across along the way. Eventually, they all found their way into the design.

This is the first version of the website, and I know it will probably change as I keep experimenting with it. But for now, I like that it is a little loud, a little unconventional, and hopefully a little more memorable than another perfectly polished website. I did not want it to look like everything else. I wanted it to feel like mine.

Why This Website?

I wanted a personal space where I could share more than just my professional work. This blog is primarily about personal reflections, experiences, and the little things that shape how I see the world, with some technology and creative explorations along the way.

I like keeping things personal and expressive, so the blog may include a little humor, random thoughts, and the occasional meme. Not everything needs to be serious all the time!

If you would like to get in touch, feel free to reach out via my email. I am always open to feedback, conversations, or new ideas!

I also maintain a separate portfolio for my projects. You can find the GitHub repository for this blog linked in the navigation bar if you would like to explore how it was built or share suggestions.

How I Organized It All

The blog is organized into categories to make exploring different topics easier. Posts are sorted by date, with the newest ones appearing first. I will keep adding new posts and updating the blog as it grows.
